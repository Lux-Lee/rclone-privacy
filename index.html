Privacy Policy

This page covers a personal tool named rclone, configured by its owner (Lux-Lee) to sync files between the owner's own Microsoft OneDrive and Google Drive accounts.

The tool is not a public service. It does not collect, store, or share personal data from anyone other than the owner. It runs only on the owner's own computer.

Google and Microsoft sign-in is handled directly by rclone on the owner's computer. The tokens it receives are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration file.
